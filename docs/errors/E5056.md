# GIONG-E5056: the service reports a warning

> giong 1.5.5 · https://giong.run/docs/errors/E5056/ · source: https://github.com/giong-run/Giong/blob/v1.5.5/docs/errors/E5056.md

Raised by the `giong` command (giong-cli): `giong top` and the dashboard. In JSON output: `"code": "GIONG-E5056"`.

## What happened

giong-mcp's `overview` listed a warning other than a stuck worker: workers that fell back to the epoll driver because io_uring is blocked, or a process close to its open-files limit. The detail is the warning's text.

## How to fix it

- Read the detail. For epoll: see [GIONG-E0001](https://giong.run/docs/errors/E0001.md) and `giong doctor`. For open files: raise the limit (`ulimit -n`, `LimitNOFILE=` in a systemd unit, or the container's `nofile` ulimit) or find the leak (`giong top` lists tasks by spawn line).
